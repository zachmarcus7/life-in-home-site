Nancy Shannon

Life in Home Team · Portland, OR

With 35+ years of real estate experience and more than 500 closed transactions, I believe real estate is about so much more than buying and selling homes. As a second-generation REALTOR® and founder of Life In Home Team Realty, I proudly serve clients throughout the Portland Metro area and beyond, from the Oregon Coast to Mt. Hood, north to the Washington border, and south to Silverton. I specialize in helping buyers, sellers, seniors, downsizers, families, and investors achieve their goals with confidence. My clients benefit from extensive market knowledge, strong negotiation skills, strategic marketing, attention to detail, and consistent communication. Throughout my career, my sellers have achieved an average of 98% or more of their original list price, with homes spending less than eight days on the market before receiving an offer or multiple offers. I also hold the SRES® (Seniors Real Estate Specialist®) and ABR® (Accredited Buyer’s Representative) designations and am a Mobile Notary.

Portland is more than the place I work. It’s my home and community. I love living in Southeast Portland and enjoying everything the Pacific Northwest has to offer. I’m the proud mother of two adult sons, a happy grandmother, gardener, animal lover, reader, and outdoor enthusiast. After more than three decades in real estate, I still genuinely love helping people navigate life’s important transitions. Whether I’m handing the keys to a first-time homebuyer, helping a family move into their next chapter, assisting seniors with downsizing, or guiding a seller through the sale of a home filled with memories, I consider it a privilege to be part of such an important decision. A home is more than a house. It’s where life happens, and I’m here to help you find the right place for your life.
