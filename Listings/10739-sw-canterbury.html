About This Property

Gorgeous townhouse conveniently located in desirable Tigard! Beautifully updated and move-in ready, this stunning townhouse showcases soaring ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, luxurious brand-new carpet, and an elegant designer color palette throughout.

The light-filled living room features a warm and inviting gas fireplace, while the well-appointed kitchen offers Whirlpool appliances and abundant cabinetry. Step outside to your private patio overlooking peaceful, park-like greenspace — the perfect setting to relax or entertain.

Completing the home is an attached tandem garage with generous storage space, providing both convenience and functionality.
